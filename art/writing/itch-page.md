# Kiln & Keep

## Tagline

Shape a little clay into a castle where everyone belongs.

## Description

Welcome to Kiln & Keep, a pottery game about building a castle one visitor at a time. You are the royal potter, opening your courtyard workshop for a day of muddy hands and warm company.

Use your thumb to shape clay on the spinning wheel. Follow each visitor's wish, choose a glaze, then fire your creation in the kiln. Every finished tower joins the growing castle, from a cosy round keep to a slender spire.

Clay collapsed? Sploosh! Your customers laugh kindly, and there is no game over. Take your time and try again.

Made for the Castles theme, this kingdom is built from pottery and hospitality. Stay until evening: the final customer needs somewhere to tuck a dragon's tail.
