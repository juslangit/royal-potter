# Brief 01 — Style sheet (key image + palette)

You are the art and writing lead for a mobile game jam entry. Claude Code builds the
mechanics in Godot; you make the creative work. The human director (Luqman) approves.

## The game
Working title: "Royal Potter". Jam theme: **Castles**.
A calm, cosy, funny pottery game for phones (portrait). You are the royal potter.
Castle folk (the king, the queen, a knight, a wizard, etc.) each order a **castle tower**
— a round watchtower, a squat keep, a turret, an onion dome, a spire — which you throw on
a spinning potter's wheel with one thumb, glaze, and fire in a kiln. Every finished tower is
placed onto the castle, so the castle grows out of your pottery over one day (morning to
evening). The last customer is a dragon. Clay can collapse ("sploosh") for laughs; there is
no game over.

## The look (locked)
- **Toon low-poly 3D**: flat colours, light in 2–3 hard bands, thick dark outlines
  (#2a2420), soft warm sunlight, nothing over-saturated.
- Warm, cosy, storybook. Terracotta clay, cream plaster, warm wood, sage green, sky blue,
  kiln-gold, rose.
- Castles made of glazed pottery: towers are visibly thrown on a wheel (ring marks,
  glossy glaze, a rim at the top), with little flags.
- Portrait composition, 9:16.

## Deliverables (save into this folder, art/concept/)
1. `key-art.png` — ONE key image, 1080x1920 portrait: the potter's workshop on a castle
   courtyard; potter's wheel in the lower third with a half-thrown tower of wet clay; behind
   it, a small castle built from glazed pottery towers. No text in the image.
2. `palette.md` — 8 named colours with hex codes, drawn from the key art, and 6 glaze
   colours for the player to pick from.
3. `style-notes.md` — 10 short bullet rules another artist (or a Blender script) must
   follow so every later asset matches the key art (shapes, outline, lighting, proportions,
   level of detail).
4. `names.md` — 10 candidate game names (English, short, fun, castle + pottery), with one
   line each. Mark your top 3.

All text in English. Do not write any game code.
