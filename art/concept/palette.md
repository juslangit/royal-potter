## Six player glaze colours

These reuse the scene palette so any combination belongs in the same castle.
Their names are the proposed English labels for the glaze picker.

| Glaze | Hex | Character |
|---|---|---|
| Cream Crown | `#F0D6AE` | Warm ivory, like the castle walls |
| Sage Keep | `#959B68` | Gentle green, like the round watchtower |
| Sky Spire | `#7BA2DA` | Clear blue, echoing the sky and blue dome |
| Honey Hall | `#F2B345` | Golden yellow, used sparingly for warmth |
| Rose Turret | `#B2675D` | Muted rose, echoing the royal banners |
| Terracotta Throne | `#AD5C37` | Earthy red-brown with a fired gloss |

Wet clay always starts as Wet Terracotta. Even when the player chooses Terracotta
Throne, firing changes its broad wet sheen into small, crisp glaze highlights.
Keep highlights cream-tinted and preserve visible throwing rings beneath every glaze.
