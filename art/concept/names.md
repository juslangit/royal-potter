# Royal Potter — ten name candidates

Creative candidates for Luqman's choice; Royal Potter remains the working title.

1. **Kiln & Keep — TOP 3, first choice.** A warm little kiln grows a proud little castle; short, rhythmic and tied to both halves of play.
2. **Royal Potter — TOP 3.** Clearly names your cosy new job: making towers for a very particular royal household.
3. **Throw a Throne — TOP 3.** A playful pottery pun that gives the royal commissions a cheeky voice.
4. **Clay Castle.** A simple, friendly promise: everything in your castle begins as a lump of clay.
5. **Glaze the Keep.** An inviting command that puts colourful pottery at the heart of castle building.
6. **Crown & Clay.** Pairs royal grandeur with the humble, messy material in your hands.
7. **Pottery Kingdom.** Suggests a whole tiny realm growing from the pots you make.
8. **The Royal Kiln.** Makes the workshop feel like the warmest, busiest corner of the castle.
9. **Turret Thrower.** Turns a castle tower into a pottery joke, with a little mock-heroic swagger.
10. **Sploosh Castle.** Celebrates wobbly clay and funny collapses in a kingdom with no game over.
