# Royal Potter — asset style rules

Match [key-art.png](key-art.png) and use the base colours in [palette.md](palette.md).
This is the proposed art reference for Luqman's review.

- **Shape:** Build chunky, lightly faceted volumes; start round tower bodies with 12–16 radial sides and avoid razor-sharp edges.
- **Silhouette:** Distinguish watchtower, squat keep, turret, onion dome and spire by their outer shape before adding decoration.
- **Proportion:** Keep most tower bodies 1–2.5 times their diameter in height; exaggerate rims, battlements and roof overhangs for phone readability.
- **Pottery:** Give every tower 3–6 broad horizontal throwing rings, a thick lip and slight handmade asymmetry; reserve masonry blocks for courtyard and kiln.
- **Outline:** Use warm dark `#2A2420` contours, about 4–6 pixels on foreground silhouettes at 1080-pixel width, with lighter internal detail.
- **Shading:** Use flat base colours and only 2–3 hard light bands per material; let polygon facets show without noisy surface textures.
- **Lighting:** Place one warm sun above the upper left, with gentle warm fill and readable contact shadows beneath props and rims.
- **Materials:** Give wet clay broad broken streaks of sheen, fired glaze small cream highlight patches, and wood or plaster a matte finish.
- **Detail:** Limit each tower to a few deep arched windows and one small flag; simplify leaves, tools and wood grain into broad shapes.
- **Composition:** Work in 9:16 portrait with the wheel in the lower third, the wet tower above it and a clearly separated pottery castle behind.
